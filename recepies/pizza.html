Pizza

Pizza is a globally popular Italian dish consisting of a flattened, leavened wheat-based dough topped with various ingredients and baked at high temperatures. It is celebrated for its perfect contrast of textures and rich, savory flavor profiles.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Yeast
  • Water
  • Olive Oil
  • Sugar
  • Salt
  • Sauce
  • Cheese

Steps

  1. Activate the yeast
  2. Knead the dough
  3. Let it Rise
  4. Preheat the oven
  5. Shape the crust
  6. Assemble and bake
