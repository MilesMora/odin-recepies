Lasagna

Lasagna is a classic Italian baked dish made by stacking wide, flat pasta sheets with alternating layers of savory sauce (usually meat ragù and creamy béchamel), various cheeses (like mozzarella, ricotta, and Parmesan), and seasonings, baked until bubbling and browned

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Sausage and ground beef
  • Crushed tomatoes and tomato paste
  • Sugar
  • Cheese
  • Egg

Steps

  1. Boil the pasta
  2. Prepare the ingredients
  3. Make the layers
  4. Rest before slicing
