Maggi

Maggi is an internationally renowned brand of instant noodles, seasonings, and soups owned by Nestlé Global. Originally founded in Switzerland in 1884, it is universally celebrated for its flagship product: quick-cooking, highly-spiced instant noodles that can be prepared in just a few minutes

Ingredients

  • Maggi packet
  • Water

Steps

  1. Pour water in a container and put the maggi into the water and boil it
  2. Pour the sachet into the container and wait until water is boiled
  3. Take the noodles out of the container and enjoy
